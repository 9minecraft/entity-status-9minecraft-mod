# Entity Status — Track Enemy Health and Stats in Real Time

Entity Status 9minecraft Mod adds a clean heads-up display that shows the health and stats of any mob you look at. When you aim your crosshair at a mob, a panel pops up on your screen with its name, icon, armor, and exact health values. The mod also displays floating health bars above mobs in the world so you can quickly see who is hurt. It includes visible potion effect icons, floating damage numbers, and boss mob support.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **Tslat**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
